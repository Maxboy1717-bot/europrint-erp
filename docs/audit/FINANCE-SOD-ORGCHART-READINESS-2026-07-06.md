# EuroPrint ERP — Finance SoD Org-Chart Readiness Check (read-only)

**Date:** 2026-07-06
**Question:** Is the org chart (`org_departments` + related tables) ready *today* to derive finance Separation-of-Duties (maker = who can create, checker = who can approve) from **org-chart structure** (department membership + hierarchy position + department head) rather than a flat manually-assigned role flag?
**Method:** Live read-only queries against `postgres@localhost:5432/europrint` (`_audit/q.cjs`) + guard/service source citations. **Investigation only — no design, no fix, no data change.**

> **One-line answer: NOT ready today.** The *mechanism* to walk the org tree to a department head already exists and works elsewhere (ZVS approval chain). The blocker is the finance **org data**: the Finance card is duplicated across two parallel subtrees, and — critically — the cards that have a **head** have **zero members**, while the cards that have **members** have a **null head**. So no finance member's card currently resolves to a head. These are owner-decision/data gaps, not missing-code gaps.

## Plain readiness verdict

**The org chart is NOT ready today to drive finance SoD from structure — but the gap is data, not mechanism.**

**The mechanism is already there and proven.** The ZVS/procurement approval chains already walk `org_departments.parent_id` recursively and read `head_user_id` to find the approver, with a clean role-based fallback when the head is missing (Q7). Building "maker = department member, checker = department head (or nearest ancestor head)" would follow an existing, working pattern — no new architecture required.

**What is missing (exactly, in owner-actionable terms):**

1. **The Finance card is duplicated and has no canonical choice.** Five finance/accounting cards exist across two parallel subtrees (Q1): a head-bearing pair (26 "Moliya" director → 27 "Buxgalteriya" section) and a member-bearing pair/trio (51 "Moliya", 60 "Buxgalteriya", plus 42/159 "Moliya bo'linmasi"). **The owner must decide which card(s) are the real Finance department** before membership or headship can be trusted.

2. **Members and heads are on different (twin) cards — the decisive blocker.** Every finance *member* sits on a card with `head_user_id = null` (51, 60), while every finance *head* sits on a card with **zero members** (26, 27). The same two people (Saida, Lola) are simultaneously a head on one card and a member of its duplicate. So a structural query "find the head of the department this finance user belongs to" returns **null for all three finance staff today**, even though the heads exist elsewhere in the tree. This must be reconciled (merge the twins, or move the head onto the member-bearing card) — an **owner data decision**.

3. **Department/otdeleniye-level heads are 0% filled.** `head_user_id` is populated only on `director`/`section`/`ceo`/`owner` nodes (18/143 total); it is `null` on **all 18 `department` and all 14 `otdeleniye` cards** (Q4). A Finance department at the `department`/`otdeleniye` level therefore has no head at its own level and, for the finance case, no parent-chain fallback head either.

4. **Membership linkage is itself multi-world** (secondary, but affects "who counts as a member"). Membership can be recorded via `users.org_department_id`, `users.card_id`, `employees.org_department_id`, `employee_cards.card_id`, or `org_functions.department_id`. Live, only `org_department_id` is populated (31 users / 3 finance employees); `card_id` has just 1 user; `employee_cards`/`org_functions.department_id` are empty for finance. **The owner/team must confirm which column is the canonical membership source** so "who is a finance maker" is well-defined.

5. **The current maker role is unassignable** (Q6): `FINANCE_OFFICER` (and `FINANCE_MANAGER`) have zero live users, so today finance create/approve collapses to `super_admin`/`director`. This is the very problem the org-derived approach would replace — noted here as the baseline, not as something to fix in this pass.

**Not blocking / already in your favor:** the `head_user_id` column exists and is *not* empty for finance — the heads (Saida on 26, Lola on 27) are real data, just attached to the member-less twin cards; and the recursive-head mechanism (Q7) is live and safe-by-fallback. So this is a **reconcile-the-data** situation (pick canonical cards, put members and a head on the same card, fill department-level `head_user_id`), after which the existing ZVS-style pattern could derive finance SoD structurally.

*(Per instructions, no design or fix is proposed here — that is the separate later step once this readiness picture is confirmed.)*
